# Getting help

Email Synauson at support@synauson.com, or use the contact you received with your
license key.

To report a problem, please include:

- the example and the release tag you used, and your operating system;
- when you ran on the host, your Java version and your GStreamer version
  (`gst-inspect-1.0 --version`);
- what you did, what you expected and what happened;
- the example's output with `SYNAUSON_LOG_LEVEL=debug` set.

Each example's README has a troubleshooting section, and jsyn's
[troubleshooting guide](https://github.com/synauson/jsyn/blob/v1.7.0/docs/troubleshooting.md)
covers the errors jsyn itself reports.
